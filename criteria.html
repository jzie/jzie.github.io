How to read the table

Each small square in the history column is one test run, oldest on the left: green passed, red failed. First passed is when a criterion first met its threshold. Passing since is when its current unbroken run of passes began. If those two dates differ, something broke along the way and was fixed. A criterion that passed and then failed shows as Regressed.

A gap this table exposed

When the criteria were first laid out, 1 and 5 had no phase that could test them: both depend on a safety layer that owns the regulated warning lights, and nothing in the plan built one. Two must-pass criteria with no route to a result is a plan that cannot answer its own question, so Phase 7 was added on Day 3 to build it.
