Field Notes: Android Automotive on a motorcycle

What changes when you take a platform built for cars and put it on a vehicle with one screen, no way to interact while moving, and warning lights the law requires to work instantly. Written for anyone at a manufacturer weighing the same question, and updated every session of the build.

How to read this

Every entry carries one of three labels, so nothing here reads as more certain than it is.

  • Confirmed  I saw it myself on this setup, and the linked session note shows how.
  • Documented  Stated in Google's own documentation or another public source.
  • Assumed  My reasoning, not yet tested. Treat with suspicion.

Everything here comes from public sources and my own motorcycle. Nothing internal to any manufacturer.

1. The differences that matter

DifferenceWhat it means on a motorcycleBasis
One display, not twoThe platform assumes a central touchscreen plus a cluster behind the wheel. A motorcycle has a cluster and nothing else, so everything the platform does with a main screen has nowhere to go. Day 2Confirmed
Nothing to touch while movingA car driver can safely touch a screen at a red light. A rider wears gloves, has both hands loaded and has no passenger to hand things to. Any interaction that is not available while parked is effectively unavailable.Assumed
Warning lights are legally required, and instantTurn signals, high beam, neutral, oil pressure, ABS and the engine light must appear the moment the key turns. Android takes tens of seconds to start, so it cannot be the only thing rendering that screen.Documented
Android is not a safety-certified systemAndroid Automotive holds no automotive safety certification such as ISO 26262, and manufacturers isolate it from safety-critical functions, often running it alongside a real-time operating system. Google's own architecture splits the cluster: a high availability renderer draws the safety and regulatory elements, and the Android layer draws the rest.Documented
The data model is car-shapedGear arrives as park, reverse, neutral and drive. A motorcycle has neutral and six gears. Lean angle, chain condition and similar do not exist in the platform at all. Day 2Confirmed
The environment is harsherDirect sunlight, rain, engine vibration and temperatures from freezing to baking, with no cabin around any of it. All of it affects readability, mounting and component life.Assumed
Every euro is fought overA cluster is a far larger share of the bill of materials on a €6,000 motorcycle than on a €40,000 car, and there is less electrical power to spare.Assumed

2. Changes we made

ChangeWhy, and what it costBasis
Treated the single display as the whole clusterRather than the platform cluster APIs, which assume a second screen, we run one full-screen app. Cheaper and simpler, and it costs us the platform's own cluster integration. DecisionConfirmed
Built a custom layout instead of Google's templatesThe Car App Library gives driver-distraction checks for free but only allows lists, grids and navigation screens. A cluster is a few very large numbers, so we took the custom route and the responsibility that comes with it. DecisionConfirmed
Converted units in the display layerThe platform stores speed in metres per second and the app decides what the rider sees. Keeping that split means a change of units never touches the data path. Day 1Confirmed
Subscribed to values rather than pollingLower power, faster updates, and the current value arrives on subscribing, so the cluster is populated at power-on rather than blank. DecisionConfirmed

3. Limitations found

LimitationDetailBasis
Gear cannot express a motorcycle gearboxThe standard gear value has no way to say third of six. Day 2Confirmed
No motorcycle-specific values existNothing for lean angle, or anything else particular to two wheels. Custom vendor values are the only route.Documented
A frozen value looks like a steady oneThe platform delivers changes, so silence and stillness are indistinguishable. On a cluster that is dangerous.Confirmed
Cluster APIs assume a second displayThe platform's cluster path expects the manufacturer to provide a service driving a secondary display, behind a dedicated permission. On a one-screen vehicle that design does not fit.Documented
Start-up time cannot meet the key-turn expectationNot yet measured on real hardware. Currently the single biggest untested risk in the project.Assumed

4. Workarounds

WorkaroundWhat it doesLimitation it answersStatus
Split the clusterA small, instant safety layer owns the regulated warning lights and Android owns everything else. This mirrors how the industry already handles it, and it is the heart of the feasibility question.Answers the start-up and safety limitationsPlanned
Custom vendor values for motorcycle dataDefine our own properties in the vendor range for gear, RPM and the rest, which requires a custom Android build rather than a stock image.Answers the car-shaped data modelPhase 3
Staleness timeout in the appBlank a value when it is older than a set age, rather than showing a number that stopped being true.Answers the frozen value problemPlanned
Start by itself at power-onNobody taps an icon on a motorcycle, so the cluster app has to launch itself and stay up.Answers the no-interaction constraintPlanned
