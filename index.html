Building an Android Automotive display for my motorcycle

I'm learning how Android Automotive OS moves vehicle data from hardware to a screen. The finish line is a working prototype showing live RPM and gear from my 2026 Yamaha R3. This site holds the session notes and every product decision along the way.

Why a product manager is doing this

I've worked on connected-vehicle products at the system level. Building one end to end teaches the constraints that shape a roadmap: what the platform gives you for free, where data can go wrong, and what it takes to keep a rider safe. Those are the conversations I want to have with engineering teams from real experience, not slides.

How it works

In Android Automotive, apps never read vehicle hardware directly. Every value passes through two platform layers first. Today the input is a slider in the emulator. Later it will be my bike.

Safety rules I've set

  • Read-only on the bike. The bridge only listens to the motorcycle's data and never sends anything onto it.
  • Glanceable, not distracting. No animations, notifications or dense text while riding.
  • Check before mounting. Road rules for displays, helmet approval and warranty impact get checked before anything goes on the bike.
