A product manager's feasibility study

Can Android Automotive run a motorcycle instrument cluster?

Android Automotive OS was built for cars. I'm finding out hands-on what it takes to run it as a motorcycle instrument cluster, and where the boundaries are, by building a working prototype fed by live data from my 2026 Yamaha R3. A personal project, approached the way a manufacturer would evaluate it. This site holds the session notes and every product decision along the way.

The question I'm answering

Android Automotive is spreading through the car industry and has started appearing on two-wheelers, but a motorcycle is not a small car. It has one screen instead of two, no way to interact while riding, a rider wearing gloves in the rain, and a cluster whose warning lights are legally required to work the instant the key turns. Android holds no automotive safety certification, so it cannot be the only thing on that screen.

So the real question is not whether Android can show a speed number. It is where the boundary sits between a safety layer that owns the regulated warning lights and an Android layer that owns everything else, and whether what's left is worth the cost, the power and the start-up time on a vehicle where every euro of the bill of materials counts.

I've worked on connected-vehicle products at the system level. Building one end to end teaches the constraints that shape a roadmap, which is the point of doing it myself rather than reading about it.

How it works

In Android Automotive, apps never read vehicle hardware directly. Every value passes through two platform layers first, and the platform checks the app's permissions on the way. Today the input is a slider in the emulator. Later it will be my bike.

Rules I've set

  • Read-only on the bike. The bridge only listens to the motorcycle's data and never sends anything onto it.
  • Glanceable, with nothing to touch. No animations, notifications or dense text, and no interaction at all while moving.
  • Android is not the safety layer. Regulated warning lights belong to a layer that starts instantly and cannot depend on Android being up.
  • Check before mounting. Road rules for displays, helmet approval and warranty impact get checked before anything goes on the bike.
